Visual Guide to Our Grass Types

Choose the exact texture, durability, and shade ideal for your residential villa, modern house, or farmhouse.

Most Popular

Korean Grass (Zoysia)

Ultra-dense, velvety soft carpet grass with low water requirements and rich green aesthetic. Perfect for luxury house gardens and modern villas.

  • Extremely soft leaf texture
  • Slow growing & low maintenance
  • High shade & heat tolerance

Fine Grass

Classic fine-bladed green lawn grass offering lush density and rapid root establishment for everyday home gardens.

  • Vibrant emerald green color
  • Quick recovery from foot traffic
  • Ideal for family play lawns

Dhaka Grass

Hardy, high-durability grass variety specifically suited for large farmhouses, park spaces, and high-sun exposure fields.

  • Heavy foot traffic resistance
  • Rapid full-coverage growth
  • Cost-effective for large grounds
